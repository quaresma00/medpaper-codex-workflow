# Medical writing at the point of drafting

Read this short guide once at S08/S09/S14/S16/S17 and reuse the relevant section.
Start from the actual close-paper observations, approved study definitions, accepted tables
and figures, and the source facts for the paragraph. Operational records stay outside the
paper. Read tool/schema documentation only when executing that operation or resolving a defect;
do not reload all quality references before each paragraph. Existing source/approval checks
still run. Examples below teach expression, not study design, factual content or mandatory form.

## Methods

Tell readers how the design answers the question: design/setting and period; participants
or source records and selection; exposure/procedure/measurements; outcomes; statistical
analysis. Merge or rearrange these for the real design. Quantitative Methods usually ends
with Statistical analysis, including the actual missing-data, subgroup and sensitivity work.
Retain eligibility, main measurement and analysis in the main text. Put lengthy code lists,
implementation details or secondary specifications in supplementary Methods; tabular content
belongs in supplementary tables. Consult methods-structure.md only if this boundary is unclear.

An original, number-free illustration (use only methods actually performed):

> We conducted a retrospective cohort study of adults receiving maintenance haemodialysis.
> Eligible patients were identified from treatment records, and follow-up began at the first
> qualifying visit. The primary outcome was hospital admission for heart failure. We used a
> Cox proportional hazards model to estimate the association between baseline serum phosphate
> and the outcome, adjusting for the prespecified clinical covariates. The handling of missing
> covariates and assessment of the proportional hazards assumption are described below.

This illustration does not license claiming prespecification, an assessment or a model that
the actual study lacks. Software names/versions and necessary statistical terminology are normal.

## Results

Read the finished tables/figures first. Open with the eligible population or records and
the final analysed units, then the principal outcome and comparison. Carry the main result
with its effect measure and interval where appropriate; give units, denominator/time horizon
and adjustment when needed. Use descriptive summaries for descriptive questions, not an
invented effect estimate. Report important null/adverse findings. Supplementary details may
be concise, but no prespecified outcome disappears. Cite displays without transcribing rows.

Useful sentence shapes, filled only from the corresponding source facts:

- Population: “Of [eligible units], [analysed units] met the inclusion criteria (Figure 1).”
- Ratio: “[Exposure] was associated with [outcome] compared with [reference] (adjusted
  [measure], [estimate]; [interval level] CI, [lower] to [upper]; Table 2).”
- Difference: “The [outcome] difference between [groups] was [estimate] [unit] ([interval
  level] CI, [lower] to [upper]) at [time].”
- Imprecision: “The estimate was [estimate] ([interval]); the interval included [null].”

Original synthetic example; **all numbers are invented teaching values, never project facts**:

> Of 920 adults assessed for eligibility, 800 were included in the analysis (Figure 1).
> During follow-up, 62 patients were admitted for heart failure. Higher baseline serum
> phosphate was associated with admission after adjustment for the specified covariates
> (hazard ratio per 1-mg/dL increase, 1.34; 95% CI, 1.02 to 1.76; Table 2).

Real prose must use the actual exposure scale, events, follow-up and adjustment. Never copy
the example's values, clinical story, causal implication or significance classification.

## Introduction

Move from the concrete medical problem and established evidence to the specific unresolved
question and the study objective. Paragraph count follows that argument and the comparators.
Each citation supports its actual claim. A source title or a stock “growing body” sentence
does not establish a gap. Explain why the question matters without asserting novelty unchecked.

## Discussion

First prepare the display-free Discussion packet and a short six-point outline through
the S16 procedure. The writer receives narrative principal findings, actual literature
comparisons, plausible explanations, clinical implications and limitations. It does not
receive this study's Results file, result tables, figure/table identifiers or internal
analysis/revision logs. Keep those for the executor's separate factual check after drafting.
Use a clean writing context; a single scoped writer may use the packet, outline and necessary
published-source passages without inheriting the full analysis conversation.

The outline covers the principal finding; comparison and reasons for differences; possible
mechanisms; clinical meaning; limitations and bias direction; conclusion. These are short
working points, **not six compulsory subheadings in the paper**. A supported explanation
that a mechanism cannot be tested or a net bias direction cannot be determined is appropriate.
Resolve missing source-based notes rather than generating a generic biological story.

Use connected paragraphs: principal finding and uncertainty; comparison with genuinely read
studies; plausible interpretation distinguished from evidence; limitations and implications.
Subheadings are optional when the design/target examples justify them. Prior-study numbers
need exact source passages; own-study numbers must remain the accepted findings. Bias direction
is stated only when defensible. An observational association alone does not establish utility.

Original illustration continuing the synthetic example:

> Higher serum phosphate was associated with heart-failure admission in this cohort.
> This finding should be interpreted in the context of the observational design: phosphate
> concentration may also reflect nutritional status and disease severity. Residual confounding
> and changes in phosphate during follow-up could not be excluded. The association therefore
> does not establish that lowering phosphate would prevent admission.

Replace this argument with the study's real evidence and cite actual prior work where used.
Do not add generic limitations absent from the design or pretend an unmeasured bias was tested.
Do not turn the Discussion into a walk through supplementary figures/tables. A few necessary
references can clarify interpretation; repeated long Results sentences and numerical
recapitulation need to become a source-supported argument. The S16 diagnostic checks clear
copying and display-reference concentration, not whether the discussion is medically adequate.

## Abstract and title

At S17 select the strongest accurate title automatically, then distil the accepted manuscript.
For quantitative original research, Background/Objectives, Methods, Results and Conclusions
are a useful starting structure. Diagnostic/prediction research must identify validation
setting and relevant performance, not imply clinical effectiveness; descriptive/case-series
abstracts use their actual design; evidence syntheses identify included studies and synthesis;
qualitative work reports sampling, analysis and principal themes. These are alternatives,
not a reporting-checklist catalogue or a forced universal template. After selection the
current journal's actual headings/word cap take precedence in its integration copy.
The abstract contains the actual population, design and principal magnitude/uncertainty,
including material null results; it introduces no new analysis. Keywords follow the abstract.

## Read once as a medical reader, then check the sources

For the completed section ask: Who/what was studied? Compared with what? Which outcome,
scale, denominator and time? What magnitude and uncertainty? What can reasonably be inferred?
Keep patients, specimens, reports and events distinct, including different source populations.
Methods/Results generally describe completed work in the past; established knowledge and
interpretation can use the present. Mixed tense or different subject labels are not automatic
errors. “Significant” alone is not a result, and a CI containing the null is not proof of no
association. A scanner can prompt these questions; it cannot answer them or fabricate an interval.
Use the existing section check and executor acceptance; create no extra report or audit round.
At display production and final review, consult medical-reader-presentation.md for whole-table,
whole-figure, legend and actual-page reading. Source bindings remain in their existing records.
